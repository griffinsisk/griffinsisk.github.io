## About

Griffin Sisk is a Senior Solutions Engineer at CloudZero, where he helps large
companies understand and control what they spend on cloud and AI. Day to day
that means sitting with engineering, finance, and AI platform teams as they
work to visualize and understand how Claude, OpenAI, Cursor, Copilot, and other
AI usage applies back to the people and products driving it, alongside the
outcomes they're delivering. Six years of technical pre-sales at VMware,
Broadcom, and CloudZero taught him how to earn trust with engineers, translate
complexity for executives, and close competitive deals. More recently he has
learned the hardest problems in enterprise AI sit outside the models, in
trustworthy data, proper context, and numbers people will actually act on.

The pattern in his best work is the same whether he is selling or building:
get into the customer's real data fast, be honest about what works and what
doesn't, and ship something they can use this week. That approach has cleared
compliance blockers other vendors walked away from, turned AI cost visibility
into a reason for executives to buy, and cut CloudZero's evaluation cycle in
half. He is looking for a role where he can do more of exactly that, embedded
with customers at a company building the AI frontier.

Interests outside work: travel, health and wellness, AI and technology,
photography and videography, animal welfare. Education: University of New
Hampshire (2014–2018), Bachelor of Business and Communications; played Men's
D1 Rugby.
